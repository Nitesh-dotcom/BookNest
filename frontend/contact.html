Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about BookNest

How long does shipping take?
Standard shipping takes 5–7 business days and is free on orders over $50. Express shipping (2–3 business days) is available for $9.99. Orders are usually processed and dispatched within 1 business day.
What is your return policy?
We accept returns within 30 days of delivery for unread books in their original condition. Simply contact us at hello@booknest.com with your order number and we'll arrange a return label at no cost to you.
Do you ship internationally?
Currently we ship within the United States only. We're actively expanding our international shipping options and expect to serve Canada and the UK in early 2025. Sign up for our newsletter to be notified when we go global!
How do I track my order?
Once you're signed in, visit My Orders in your account dashboard to see the current status of all your orders. You'll also receive email updates at each stage of your delivery.
Can I cancel my order?
You can cancel an order within 24 hours of placing it, provided it hasn't been shipped yet. Go to My Orders and click the Cancel button on any PENDING or CONFIRMED order. After shipping, please use our returns process.
Do you offer gift wrapping?
Yes! Gift wrapping is available at checkout for $2.99 per item. We use beautiful, eco-friendly kraft paper wrapping with a personalised gift note card. Perfect for birthdays, holidays, and any occasion that calls for a great book.
